What happens after you send the form?

A typical agency intake asks for project details, files and access. Once it is sent, somebody still needs to check what arrived, work out what is missing, follow up with the client and prepare the information for the team doing the work.

You can build that workflow around a form yourself. Clientovo is another option: a portal supported by a team handling collection, follow-ups, review and handover. It is not just a different question editor.

This is a comparison of workflows, not a claim that form tools cannot be extended with integrations. What matters is who owns the ongoing work and whether your agency wants to manage it.

When a form may be all you need

If your intake is short, clients tend to respond fully and somebody already has time to check and organise submissions, a form can be a good fit. There is no need to add a managed service simply because your current form looks basic.

A self-managed approach can also suit agencies that want complete control over reminders and handover and are happy to maintain their own process. Consider the team time involved, not only the price of the form tool.

When managed onboarding is worth trying

Consider a managed service when the same problems keep recurring: missing brand files, unclear answers, information spread across messages or an account manager who repeatedly has to chase before delivery can start.

Before buying, walk through a recent intake. Note who sent reminders, who checked the responses and who organised the files. That gives you a realistic baseline to compare with a trial. There is no universal time-saving figure: it depends on your clients, template and existing workflow.

What Clientovo does—and does not do

Your agency creates its own questions, submits client details and tracks progress. Clients receive a private onboarding link and provide answers and files. Clientovo handles follow-ups and reviews the collected information before handover.

Once approved, the agency is notified by email and signs in to view or download its pack. A client completing the required fields does not automatically publish it. The agency remains responsible for its client relationship, agreed scope, platform permissions and specialist project decisions. We do not promise a client will respond by a fixed date.
