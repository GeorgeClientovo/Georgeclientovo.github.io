## Implemented

Distinct titles and descriptions, self-canonical URLs, crawlable HTML guides, homepage and process internal links, Open Graph metadata, Organization/WebSite/Service and Article/BreadcrumbList JSON-LD as applicable, and sitemap discovery for both guides. No fake reviews, ranking claims, invented search volumes, mass-generated location pages or special AI ranking tags. No change to the portal or primary navigation layout.

## Measurement still requires account access

Use Search Console queries, impressions, clicks and indexing status to refine this map. Keyword Planner data can refine demand estimates if George connects or exports it. Check the two new public URLs with Search Console URL Inspection after deployment. Do not promise indexing or a ranking position.
