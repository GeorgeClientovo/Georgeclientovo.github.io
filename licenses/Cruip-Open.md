The Clientovo implementation adapts the template header, centred hero, gradient headings, video preview, workflow cards and CTA treatment to the existing static HTML site. Local copies of the template’s Nacelle regular/semibold fonts and three decorative SVG illustrations are included. No template testimonials, customer logos or fictional product claims are used. Clientovo’s own branding and video remain separate original assets.
