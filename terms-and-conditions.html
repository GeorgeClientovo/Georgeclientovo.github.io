It is not a verification of identity, ownership, authenticity, legal compliance, technical fitness, financial accuracy or every fact in a Client's answers. Any additional verification must be expressly agreed in writing. We will still perform the agreed service with reasonable care and skill.

You remain responsible for deciding whether the information meets your project requirements and for your decisions using it. This does not exclude responsibility for our own failure to perform the agreed review properly.

3.5 We aim to hand over a reviewed pack within seven working days after onboarding starts and within three working days after the Client supplies all required answers and files in a form ready for review. These are service targets, not unconditional guarantees. The seven-day target depends on timely Client participation; we do not hand over an incomplete or unreviewed pack merely to meet it. Where review reasonably identifies missing information or a correction, we will explain what is required and the effect on the target. Onboarding starts when the Client invitation is successfully issued; final completion means the required information, including requested corrections, has been supplied.
