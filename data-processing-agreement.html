Data Processing Agreement

Not yet in force. Prepared for review on 9 October 2026. Marked decisions and provider-contract checks must be resolved before customer acceptance.

Terms and conditions · Privacy & security

1. Roles, scope and duration

1.1 This DPA applies when Clientovo processes personal data contained in Agency Client onboarding information on your behalf. For that processing you are normally the controller and Clientovo the processor. If you act as a processor for another controller, you must have authority to appoint Clientovo as a subprocessor, relay lawful instructions and disclose that arrangement to us.

1.2 Role labels do not override the facts. Clientovo acts as an independent controller for its own account administration, billing, necessary security and business records as described in its Privacy Notice; it must have its own lawful basis for those activities.

1.3 The subject matter is managed collection, review and handover of Client onboarding information. Processing includes receipt, storage, organisation, retrieval, display, reminders, authorised access, review, export and deletion. Its purpose is performing your documented onboarding instructions. It continues for the service and any agreed lawful return/deletion period.

1.4 Data subjects ordinarily include adult Clients, Agency contacts and adult business representatives identified in necessary project materials. Data ordinarily includes names, work contact details, business affiliations, questionnaire responses, uploaded project files, correspondence and progress records. Restricted categories in Part A clause 4 are outside standard scope. Any expansion requires a documented assessment and written agreement.

2. Instructions and lawful processing

2.1 We will process Agency Client personal data only on your documented lawful instructions, including concerning international transfers, unless UK law requires otherwise. Where legally permitted, we will tell you about a compulsory legal requirement before processing. Templates, authorised submissions, permitted portal actions and agreed written support instructions can document instructions within the agreed scope.

2.2 Tell us immediately about an instruction which should cease or change. We will inform you if, in our opinion, an instruction infringes applicable data-protection law and may pause the affected operation while it is resolved. We will not use Client information for independent marketing, sale or unrelated profiling.

2.3 You must establish lawful processing, provide required notices and ensure your instructions and content are necessary and appropriate. We will assist with our own obligations and reasonably cooperate; your responsibilities do not relieve us of ours.

3. Confidentiality and security

3.1 We will restrict access to authorised people who need it and are bound by confidentiality or an appropriate statutory duty. We will maintain appropriate technical and organisational measures under applicable data-protection law, taking account of the processing, foreseeable risks, state of the art and implementation costs.

3.2 The current application controls include HTTPS, protected session cookies, server-side Agency/workspace access checks, staff authenticator requirements, private file storage, short-lived authorised downloads, upload restrictions and relevant activity records. We will not materially weaken agreed safeguards without an appropriate assessment and required notice. No certification, guaranteed invulnerability or antivirus connection is represented.

Application code alone does not prove these operational measures exist.]

4. Subprocessors and overseas transfers

4.1 You generally authorise only the subprocessors recorded in the completed Schedule below for the defined processing. Before adding or replacing a subprocessor, we will give prior written notice with sufficient information and [CONFIRM: reasonable objection window] for a reasoned data-protection objection. We will consider alternative measures and, if no reasonable resolution is possible, permit termination of the affected service and fair treatment of unused paid work before the change.

4.2 We will undertake appropriate due diligence and put a binding written agreement in place imposing equivalent applicable data-protection obligations on each subprocessor. We remain responsible to you for their performance of those obligations as required by law.

4.3 We will not make a restricted international transfer without applicable lawful safeguards, required assessment and authorised instructions. Listing a provider or obtaining general acceptance of these Terms is not itself a transfer safeguard. Actual legal entities, regions, support access and transfer mechanisms must be established before the Schedule is accepted.

5. Rights requests and regulatory assistance

5.1 Taking account of the nature of processing, we will assist through appropriate measures with requests for access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and portability. If a request concerns your Client data, we will notify you promptly and not independently decide the substantive response unless legally required. We may verify identity appropriately and minimise disclosed data.

5.2 Taking account of available information and the nature of processing, we will assist you with security duties, breach notifications, impact assessments and prior consultation with a regulator where required. Any agreed charges for exceptional assistance must be reasonable and must not prevent mandatory assistance or cover correction of our own breach as if it were an optional extra.

6. Personal data breaches

6.1 We will notify you without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach involving data processed for you. Initial notice will not be postponed until every detail is known. [CONFIRM: operational contact and any achievable additional contractual response target; do not promise a deadline unsupported by staffing.]

6.2 As information becomes available, we will provide the nature of the breach, affected data/data subjects and approximate numbers where known, a contact point, likely consequences and measures taken or proposed. We will take appropriate containment and remediation steps, preserve relevant evidence and cooperate with your lawful notification obligations. We will provide updates where information is initially incomplete.

6.3 This DPA does not prevent either party making a legally required notification or relieve a controller of deciding its own regulatory and individual notifications. We will not notify your Clients on your behalf without authorisation unless law requires it.

7. Information, audits and compliance

7.1 We will make available information necessary to demonstrate compliance with applicable processor obligations and allow and contribute to audits and inspections by you or an appointed independent auditor.

7.2 Reasonable notice, confidentiality, security and scheduling arrangements may protect other customers and avoid unnecessary disruption, but must not obstruct mandatory audit rights, justified incident investigations or regulatory access. We will not disclose unrelated customers' personal data. Evidence reviews may be used where appropriate without eliminating necessary inspection rights.

8. Return, deletion and retention

8.1 At the end of relevant services, we will, at your choice, return or delete personal data processed for you and delete remaining copies, unless UK law requires retention. You may instruct lawful deletion during the service. We will cooperate with necessary scope checks so one Agency cannot delete another's information.

8.2 Completed packs do not have a fixed automatic expiry while the Agency account remains open and the Agency has an ongoing lawful need and instruction to retain them. This is not an unconditional lifetime-storage promise: storage must be reviewed, necessary and lawful; appropriate deletion instructions, applicable data-subject rights, account closure and service discontinuation still apply. You must periodically review whether continued retention is necessary and instruct deletion when it is not. We will maintain our own appropriate retention review and assistance procedures. [CONFIRM: the actual review process and treatment of long-inactive accounts before publication.]

8.2A On an authorised Agency account-closure request, we will arrange return or deletion of the Agency's Client data as you choose, revoke access and stop the relevant ongoing processing. The intended deletion scope includes active onboarding records, templates, answers, attachments, contact records and relevant queued content under our control. Deletion must also address relevant subprocessor copies. Minimal billing, tax, dispute or security records may be retained only where necessary under an applicable legal obligation or other valid basis, with access restricted to that purpose. Copies previously downloaded by the Agency remain outside our control.

8.2B Backup copies that cannot immediately be selectively erased will be protected and put beyond ordinary use until deleted through a defined appropriate rotation; restored data must not resurrect erased records into ordinary service. [CONFIRM before publication: account-closure procedure; export and deletion deadlines; actual provider retention and backup rotation maximum. The current repository review did not identify a complete Agency self-service account deletion or whole-account purge. Do not represent a button or instant all-copy wipe as already implemented.]

8.3 Client link expiry, archiving a job, cancelling a subscription and removing a portal attachment are not interchangeable with complete deletion of all personal data. Agency-controlled downloads are the Agency's responsibility. We will confirm completion of agreed deletion where reasonably requested and explain lawful exceptions.

9. Liability and survival

9.1 Each party remains responsible for its own statutory obligations. Any valid negotiated liability allocation between the parties does not bind a regulator or remove a data subject's statutory rights. Part A clause 11 applies only to the extent lawful and reasonable and consistent with this DPA. Confidentiality, lawful retention, cooperation and deletion obligations continue for as long as relevant data is held.
