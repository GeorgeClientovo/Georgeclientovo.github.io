How it works

We take the chasing off your team.

When you sign a new client, send them to Clientovo. We manage the repetitive back-and-forth until your team has what it needs to start.

01

Tell us what you need

Files, questionnaires, account access, contacts and anything else required before the project starts.

02

We collect and follow up

We guide the client through the requirements, track what is missing and chase outstanding items.

03

Your team gets the handover

Everything comes back organised so your team can stop doing onboarding admin and start the actual work.

In short: you keep the client relationship. Clientovo handles the onboarding admin.
