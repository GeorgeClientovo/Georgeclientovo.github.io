How it works

We take the chasing off your team.

Your agency uses its own secure portal to submit new clients and see how each onboarding is progressing. We run the collection and follow-ups, review the completed answers and files, then notify you when the pack is ready in your portal.

01

Create your templates

Sign in to your agency portal and make reusable templates for the information, files and access you need.

02

Add a client in your portal

Enter their name, company and email, choose your template and submit the request. Our team starts the onboarding and sends the client their private link.

03

Track progress and get the pack

See each request’s progress in your portal. We follow up on missing items, review the completed answers and files, then email your agency a link to the approved pack. A client reaching 100% does not send the pack automatically.

In short: you submit the client in your portal; we handle the collection, follow-ups and handover.
