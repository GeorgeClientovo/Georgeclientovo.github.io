Questions agencies ask

Something else on your mind? Ask us a question.

What does the free onboarding include?

One real client onboarding using your agency’s template, including collection, follow-ups, review and handover. Your new account includes one free credit. No card is needed, and you won’t be enrolled in a paid subscription.

How is this different from a form?

Your portal gives you a place to create templates and track clients. Our team handles the work around the form: collecting information, following up, reviewing responses and organising the finished pack.

Can we use our own questions and requirements?

Yes. Create reusable templates for the information, files and account access details your agency needs. Use a different template for each kind of project. Do not request passwords through an onboarding form.

Do we keep control of the client relationship?

Yes. Your agency owns the relationship and delivers the project. We handle the onboarding admin you ask us to manage, and you can track progress in your portal.

How do we receive the completed onboarding?

After our team has reviewed and approved the answers and files, we email your agency a link to the finished pack in its portal. Reaching 100% does not send the handover automatically.
