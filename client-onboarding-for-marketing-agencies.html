For marketing agencies

Client onboarding for marketing agencies.

You win the client and decide what your team needs to get started. Add them through your Clientovo agency portal; our team collects the information and files, follows up on missing items and emails you an organised handover.

See how it works ↓
A clear handoff

From signed client to ready-to-start brief.

Use the same template for repeatable work or make different templates for websites, campaigns and other services.

01

Create the template in your portal

List the questions and files your team needs before work starts. Your agency's templates stay in its own portal.

02

Submit the client

Enter the client's name, company and email, select your template and add any instructions. The request appears on our dashboard. The client is not emailed until our team starts the onboarding.

03

We collect and follow up

We start the onboarding and the client receives a private link to provide answers and upload files. We check their progress and follow up on outstanding requirements.

04

Receive the pack

Your portal shows the percentage complete and the current stage. Once we have reviewed the completed onboarding, we email the organised pack to your agency contact.

What to ask for

Build onboarding around the work you actually deliver.

These are examples for marketing projects. Your team chooses the questions and files for each template.

Brand and project brief

  • Brand guidelines, logo files and image assets
  • Goals, audience and campaign priorities
  • Existing website and marketing channels
  • Key contacts and approval process

Accounts and delivery details

  • Which analytics, ad and website platforms are in use
  • Who can grant your team access
  • Deadlines and launch requirements
  • Documents or examples your team needs

Account access: ask the client to grant access through each platform's own sharing tools. Do not ask them to put passwords into an onboarding answer.

What arrives in the handover?

An organised onboarding pack with the client's answers and uploaded files, emailed to your agency contact after review. Your portal shows progress and stage; client answers and files are handed over in the pack.

Common questions

How the managed service fits your agency.

Does the client get an email as soon as we submit them?

No. Submitting a request sends it to the Clientovo team. We check the details and start the onboarding; that is when the client receives their private link.

Can we have different templates for different services?

Yes. You create your own templates in the agency portal and select the right one when submitting each client.

Do we need to chase the client ourselves?

Clientovo follows up on missing onboarding items. Your agency can watch progress in the portal while keeping ownership of the client relationship and project delivery.

Is Clientovo just a form tool?

No. The portal gives you a way to submit clients and see progress; our team manages the collection, follow-ups, review and handover.
