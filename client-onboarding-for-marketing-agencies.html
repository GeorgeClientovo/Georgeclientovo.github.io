For marketing agencies

A practical client onboarding checklist.

A new website project can stall because the brief, brand files or account access arrive in separate email threads. Use the sample questions below to build a reusable template for your agency, then tailor it to the work you actually sell.

See the sample template ↓
Before you ask the client

Decide what your team needs to start.

A shorter, relevant form is easier for a client to finish. Agree the deliverables first, then make only genuinely blocking questions required.

1. Define the projectWebsite build, campaign, rebrand or ongoing marketing? Each may need a different template.
2. Name the decision makerKnow who supplies assets and who approves the brief, copy and final work.
3. Separate essential from optionalAsk for what you need to begin; collect nice-to-have details later.
Copy and adapt

Sample template: a new website project.

These are example question labels you can enter in your Clientovo agency portal. The suggested answer types match the portal's question options. Change them to suit the project and avoid asking for information you already have.

Project brief

Start with the outcome

  1. What should this website achieve for your business? Text · required
  2. Who is the main audience, and what should they do on the site? Text · required
  3. Which pages or features do you expect? Text · required
  4. Is there a target launch date or an event driving it? Text · optional
People and approvals

Know who can make decisions

  1. Who is our main contact for questions? Text · required
  2. Who will approve the design and copy? Text · required
  3. Is there anyone else we should involve? Text · optional
Brand and content

Gather usable assets

  1. Upload your logo file. File · required if needed
  2. Upload your brand guidelines. File · optional
  3. Where can we find approved website copy or product information? URL or text · optional
  4. Do you have images you are allowed to use on the site? Yes/No · optional

The portal accepts one file per file question. Add another file question when you need a separate asset.

Platforms and access

Use proper account sharing

  1. What is your current website URL? URL · required if applicable
  2. Which website platform and analytics tools do you use? Text · optional
  3. Have you invited our team through the platform's access-sharing settings? Describe what access was granted. Access · only if needed

Never ask clients to enter passwords. Use the platform's invitation or permissions feature.

Check the scope: a landing page, ecommerce build and full rebrand need different questions. Treat this as a starting point, not a mandatory form for every client.

Other marketing work

Swap questions when the deliverable changes.

For a campaign, you may need channels, target audience, approved creative, conversion goal and who signs off the spend. For social media work, you may need brand tone, content examples, approval contacts and access to the relevant accounts. Keep the template tied to the service.

Common questions

Make the template easy to complete.

Should every question be required?

No. Make a question required when work genuinely cannot begin without it. Keep optional details optional so a client can make progress while gathering assets.

Can we change a template later?

Yes. Your agency can edit its templates in the portal. Review a template before reusing it for a different service or project scope.

Should we ask for logins or passwords?

No. Ask the client to invite your team through the platform's own sharing flow, then use an Access question to confirm what they granted. Do not put passwords into onboarding answers.
