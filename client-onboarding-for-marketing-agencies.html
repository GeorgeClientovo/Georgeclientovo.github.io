A practical example

What might a website project template ask?

Your template depends on the service. Here are a few example questions you could add in the portal.

Text · requiredWhat should the new website achieve?

Get a clear brief before design starts.

File · requiredUpload your logo and brand assets.

The client can attach several files to one file question and add more later.

URL · optionalWhere is your current website?

Collect useful links alongside the brief.

Access · if neededHave you invited our team through the platform’s sharing settings?

Confirm access without asking anyone to enter a password.

You can make different templates for website builds, campaigns or ongoing marketing. Only mark a question required when the answer is genuinely needed to move forward.

Common questions

Before you submit your first client.

Do we email the client ourselves?

No. Submit the client in your agency portal. After reviewing the request, Clientovo emails them a private onboarding link and follows up on missing details.

Can we change a template later?

Yes. Your agency can edit its templates in the portal. Make separate templates for services that need different information.

Can the client upload multiple files?

Yes. A file question accepts several PDF, image or text files, and the client can return to add more.

When do we get the completed information?

Your portal shows progress while the client fills in their onboarding. Once we have reviewed the completed answers, we hand over the organised pack to your agency.
