The full process

What happens after you sign a new client?

Your team submits the client through its portal. Here is what happens next.

01

Set up your agency portal and templates

We give your agency a secure login. In your portal, create reusable templates with the questions, files and access details you need for each kind of client.

For example: brand guidelines, logos, project questionnaire, Google Analytics access, CMS access and key contacts.
02

Submit the new client in your portal

Enter the client's name, company and email, choose one of your templates and add any extra instructions. The request goes straight to our team for review.

03

We start the onboarding

Our team starts the job and the client receives their private onboarding link by email. They use it to provide the information and files in your template.

04

You see progress in your portal

Your agency can see its requests and onboarding progress. Our team checks what the client has provided and keeps track of anything still outstanding.

05

We follow up with the client

Our team chases missing information so your account manager does not have to keep sending follow-up emails.

06

We email your agency the handover pack

Once the onboarding is ready and reviewed, we send the organised pack to your agency's contact email so your delivery team can get started.

Common questions

A few things agencies usually want to know.

How does our agency use the portal?

Your agency signs in, creates its own templates and submits each new client's details with the right template. You can then see your requests and their progress. Our team starts the onboarding and sends the client their private link; submitting a request does not email the client immediately.

Does Clientovo replace our existing onboarding process?

No. You create templates for the information your team already needs. We take over the collection, tracking and follow-up work.

Do we lose control of the client relationship?

No. Your agency remains responsible for the client relationship and the work you're delivering. Clientovo handles the onboarding admin you choose to hand over.

What can you collect from clients?

Essentially, anything your agency needs from a client to get started. We build the onboarding process around your requirements, whether that means questionnaires, files, brand assets, project information, contacts, account access or something specific to the service you provide.

What happens if a client doesn't send something?

We keep track of the outstanding requirement and follow up with the client, rather than leaving your team to repeatedly chase it.
