The full process

What actually happens after you send us a client?

No mystery workflow. No vague software jargon. This is the process.

01

We learn what your agency needs

Before the first onboarding, you tell us what your team normally needs from a new client before work can begin. We shape the process around that.

For example: brand guidelines, logos, project questionnaire, Google Analytics access, CMS access and key contacts.
02

You send us the new client

Once you've closed the deal, you pass us the client's basic details and tell us which onboarding process they need. Your team can then get back to everything else.

03

We collect the information, files and access

Clientovo gives the client a clear route through everything your agency needs, rather than leaving requirements scattered across emails and messages.

04

We keep track of what's missing

We monitor the onboarding as it progresses. If something hasn't been provided, it stays visible as outstanding rather than quietly disappearing into somebody's inbox.

05

We follow up with the client

Outstanding items are followed up so your account manager doesn't have to keep sending “just following up on this” emails.

06

Your team gets an organised handover

Once everything has been collected, the onboarding information is handed back clearly so the people doing the actual work can get started.

Common questions

A few things agencies usually want to know.

Does Clientovo replace our existing onboarding process?

No. The service is designed around what your agency already needs. We take over the repetitive collection, tracking and follow-up work rather than forcing you into a completely different process.

Do we lose control of the client relationship?

No. Your agency remains responsible for the client relationship and the work you're delivering. Clientovo handles the onboarding admin you choose to hand over.

What can you collect from clients?

Essentially, anything your agency needs from a client to get started. We build the onboarding process around your requirements, whether that means questionnaires, files, brand assets, project information, contacts, account access or something specific to the service you provide.

What happens if a client doesn't send something?

We keep track of the outstanding requirement and follow up with the client, rather than leaving your team to repeatedly chase it.
